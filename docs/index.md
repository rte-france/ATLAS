# Atlas

**Atlas** is a powerful simulation and optimization tool developed by **Artelys** for **RTE** (Réseau de Transport d'Électricité) to model and analyze electricity markets. It enables comprehensive simulation of various market mechanisms including day-ahead markets, intraday trading, and reserve markets, providing essential insights for power system operations and planning.

Atlas uses a **workflow-based architecture** to simulate realistic market dynamics over extended periods.
